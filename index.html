Such a long day

You say you're sad. You say you're angry. Two words, and the day moves on without you.

But underneath I'm just sad is something with a real shape: a moment, a sentence you haven't said out loud yet. MindOnion helps you find it, so you can stand in front of what's actually there, not just the weather of it.

Psychologists call this affect labeling: naming the real cause of a feeling is often where it starts to loosen its grip.

Come in when you're sad. Come in when you're angry. That's exactly who this is for.

How it works

Five short questions instead of one blank page.

01

What happened?

Say it exactly as it felt. No need to make it fair, calm, or reasonable.

02

What triggered you?

The specific moment, not the whole situation.

03

What did you feel?

Anger and hurt aren't the same thing as shame or betrayal. Naming the exact one matters.

04

What did you tell yourself?

The quiet story you told yourself about what it meant.

05

What did you need?

Respect, safety, recognition: the thing underneath the reaction.
